About Insheera

Insheera is a forward-thinking software development company dedicated to creating smooth, free, and ad-free mobile applications that prioritize user experience, privacy, and performance.

We began our journey with meaningful Islamic apps like Al Muslim and Qibla Compass, which continue to serve thousands of users worldwide. However, our mission has since expanded — we now focus on building a wide range of practical, everyday tools for all users, regardless of background or belief.

From file sharing, task management, digital wallets, and productivity tools to quizzes, daily utilities, and more — we aim to simplify and enhance life through accessible, clean, and user-friendly apps.

With over 35,000 downloads across more than 20 countries, Insheera continues to grow with a commitment to quality, simplicity, and innovation in every project we launch.
